Cites Extractor

Off by default. When on, the DOI of each saved paper (or its arXiv DOI) is sent to OpenAlex (api.openalex.org) to get the number of times it is cited, and a line like Citations: 123 (OpenAlex, 2026-01-31) is added to the item. Nothing else is sent. Papers without a DOI, and PDFs added as bare files, get no count. This is the only place the add-on contacts a third-party service.
